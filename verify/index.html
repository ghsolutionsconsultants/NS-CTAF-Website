Status meanings

What each certificate status means

Active

Certificate is valid and current.

Expiring Soon

Expires within the notification period (e.g. 60 days).

Expired

Certificate validity has ended.

Suspended

Temporarily paused pending review.

Withdrawn

Certification has been removed.

Superseded

Replaced by a newer certificate or assessment.
